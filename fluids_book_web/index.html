Module Activities¶

Lectures

  • 1 ×\times× 2 hour lecture

Problem Classes

  • 1 ×\times× 50 minute problem-based `seminar’. Opportunity to ask for help.

Classes start on the hour and finish at 10 \,minutes to the hour.

Syllabus¶

  • Introduction to Fluid Properties.

    • Density

    • Pressure

    • Pressure measurement - manometry

  • Introduction to chapter-fluid_statics.

    • Submerged surfaces

    • Buoyancy

  • Introduction to chapter-fluid_motion.

    • Laminar & turbulent flow - Reynold’s number

    • Newton’s law of viscosity

    • Mass conservation - continuity

    • Energy conservation - Bernoulli’s equation

    • Momentum conservation - Euler equation

How to use these notes¶

We will go through the main notes, together with worked examples, in lectures. I would encourage you to annotate as we go through - to pick out e.g.~key words, things you find challenging, or helpful alternative explanations. For the worked examples, I suggest you write out the solutions with me, which is a great way of learning both how to set out calculations but also helps embed the learning.

After lectures - go back through the notes and test yourself by repeating the worked examples. Then move onto the exercise sheets.

Exercise sheet questions are grouped in 3 categories.

  1. 💧 Practicing a particular technique or embedding a definition; checking that you can execute those.

  2. 📝 Exam style questions using those techniques. You may need to make decisions about the appropriate techniques are to use, work to navigate your way through setting up the problem, or interpret the results.

  3. 🔥 `Challenging’ questions that go beyond exam level. These develop your interest in fluids, help you generate resilience in your understanding and problem-solving at exam level, and hopefully give you a fun mental workout!
